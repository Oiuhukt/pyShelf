14.2 Other Quantitative Approaches
We close this section with some pointers to related approaches. While in
Adams’ approach we ﬁnd a probabilistic characterization of P-entailment, the
reader may wonder whether also R-entailment can be represented by a quantitative approach. Indeed, utilizing a nonstandard probabilistic approach including
inﬁnitesimal values, Lehmann and Magidor (1992) present a variant of Adams’
system that characterizes rational entailment.
