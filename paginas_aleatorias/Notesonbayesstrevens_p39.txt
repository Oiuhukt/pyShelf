This problem, it turns out, not only undermines a certain application of
the theorem of total probability, but goes to the very root of Bayesianism,
for the reason that the whole apparatus of subjective probability assumes
that the individual puts a probability distribution over all possibilities. The
issues arising will be examined in section 11.4. Until then, put these worries
aside.
