You can format displays differently by defining your own macro, analogous to \leftdisplays. The macro definition must place a call on
\displaysetup in \everydisplay so as to ensure that \displaysetup
is called at the start of every display. The macro definition must also
include a definition of \generaldisplay.
\newtoks\previouseverydisplay
\def\leftdisplays{%
\previouseverydisplay = \everydisplay
\everydisplay =
{\the\previouseverydisplay \displaysetup}%
\def\generaldisplay{%
\leftline{%
\strut \indent \hskip\leftskip
\dimen0 = \parindent
\advance\dimen0 by \leftskip
\advance\displaywidth by -\dimen0
\@redefinealignmentdisplays
\ifeqno \ifleqno
\kern-\dimen0
\rlap{$\displaystyle\eqn$}%
\kern\dimen0
\fi\fi
$\displaystyle{\eq}$%
\ifeqno \ifleqno\else
\hfill $\displaystyle{\eqn}$%
\fi\fi}}}%
