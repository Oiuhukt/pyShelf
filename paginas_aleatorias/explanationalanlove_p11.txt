If the early Cambrian posed problems for stratigraphers, they were nothing compared to the problems posed by the Neoproterozoic (Knoll [2000]). For much of this
interval, there were no large organisms to speak of and thus no body fossils to use for
stratigraphic correlation. Large multicellular organisms make their ﬁrst appearance
in the record during the last stretch of Neoproterozoic time, today called the Ediacaran period. Still, these have limited utility for correlation and were traditionally
