Neal A. Lester

Neal A. Lester, PhD, is a Foundation Professor of English and Founding Director of Project Humanities at Arizona
State University, Tempe, AZ, USA. Lester’s research expertise in African American literary and cultural studies
ranges from U.S. race relations, popular culture, cultural appropriation, the N-word, the race and gender politics of
hair, Black masculinities, African American children’s literature, humanities and entrepreneurship, violence
against Black children, and everyday lessons in privilege and bias. Lester’s most recent book, Social Justice in Action:
Models for Campus and Community, is forthcoming from the Modern Language Association in November 2024. His
essay on Beyoncé’s “Break My Soul” was published in The Journal of Popular Culture in September 2024.
