• C2 ∶= ◻¬p ∨ ◻p ∨ ◇¬p.
Take C1 . We form two disjunctions, (1) ¬p ∨ p and (2) p ∨ p. Because (1) is a tautology, C1
passes the test.
Take C2 . We form two disjunctions, (1) ¬p∨¬p and (2) p∨¬p. Because (2) is a tautology,

Because both conjuncts pass the test, (◻¬p ∨ ◻p ∨ ◇p) ∧ (◻¬p ∨ ◻p ∨ ◇¬p) passes the
test.

the test. The wff has four conjuncts:
• C1 ∶= ◻¬p ∨ ◇¬p.
• C2 ∶= ◻¬p ∨ ◻¬q.
• C3 ∶= ◻¬q ∨ ◇¬p.
• C4 ∶= ◻¬q ∨ ◻¬q.
Take C1 . We form one disjunction, (1) ¬p ∨ ¬p. We do not need to go any further, for (1)
is not a tautology, so C1 doesn’t pass the test, so the entire wff does not pass the test.
