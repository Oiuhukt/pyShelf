This is robustly incompatible with acceptance just so long as the psychological

However, on this way of deﬁning rejection2, the objection to Beall developed in
5.5 is strengthened. A dialetheist may be in a good psychological position to know
that she rejects1 all Curry sentences or all contradictions regarding borderline
applications of vague predicates, but how can she be so sure that she
rejects2 them? If the truth operator is good for anything, it is for giving us the ability
to signal our acceptance of claims that beings with our limitations cannot express
directly. This creates the ever-present possibility of accepting claims we would not
accept if we realized that they contradicted other claims of which we are more
certain.
Put more simply, if rejection1 is compatible with acceptance, so is rejection2!
